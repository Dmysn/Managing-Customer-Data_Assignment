# Demo video script (about 2 min 45 s)

**Setup before recording:** open `dashboard/studio-north-dashboard.html` in Chrome or Edge, maximize the window, zoom to about 90%,
and drag the slider to **Jan 2024**. Record the screen with Windows **Win + Alt + R** (Xbox Game Bar) or OBS. Speak at a calm pace —
about 130 words per minute. On-screen actions are in *italics*.

---

### 0:00 – 0:20 · What this is
*Show the top of the page at Jan 2024.*

> "This is a month-by-month replay of our Studio North customer-economics report. The question is the report's question:
> are we moving in the right direction as a company? The slider at the top sets the as-of month. At each month, every chart shows only
> what Studio North could have known by that month's end — later months and cohorts that hadn't joined yet are hidden."

### 0:20 – 0:45 · How to use it
*Point at the controls; click **Next ▶** once to Feb 2024; then press the → key a few times to May 2024.*

> "You can drag the slider, click Prev and Next, press the arrow keys, or hit Play. The status line tells you what data and model are
> behind the view. In January and February 2024 the forecast is blank — there isn't enough renewal history to fit the report's model, so we
> don't pretend. From March 2024 on, the model is refit every month using only data up to that month."

### 0:45 – 1:10 · Reading the panels
*At May 2024, hover a retention-triangle cell, then hover a bar in the LTV chart.*

> "The retention triangle shows the share of each cohort still subscribed by age. In the LTV chart, the solid part of each bar is
> contribution value already observed; the hatched part is the forecast, and the black tick is acquisition cost. Observed and forecast
> are always visually separate. The payback chart uses filled dots for observed payback and hollow dots for forecasts."

### 1:10 – 1:45 · The change that matters (1): newer cohorts get better
*Step with → to Oct 2024, then to **Nov 2024**; point at the "Same-age check" panel turning green and the first filled cell in the timeline.*

> "Here's the first change that matters for management. In November 2024, for the first time, the newest cohorts with six payments beat
> our first cohorts at the same age: retention at age five rose from about 47 to 57 percent, and cash after acquisition cost went from
> minus 12 to plus 9 dollars per customer. That rule holds every month after. So the direction was clear more than a year before the
> report."

### 1:45 – 2:25 · The change that matters (2): where to spend the next dollar
*Drag to **Jul 2025** (decision table appears), then step to **Oct 2025**, **Nov 2025** and **Dec 2025**, pointing at the decision table
badges and the evidence timeline.*

> "The acquisition decision needs our randomized monthly holdouts, which first completed in July 2025. In October, Search build-skill first
> looks like the best place for the next dollar, but in November another cell briefly overtakes it — the evidence isn't stable yet. Only in
> December does Search build-skill's value of about 284 dollars beat even the upper bound of its incremental acquisition cost. That is the
> month our report recommends shifting budget there and not scaling the save-time campaigns. The lesson for management: keep the monthly
> tests running, because the spending recommendation depends on them."

### 2:25 – 2:45 · Trust and wrap-up
*Scroll to the **Reconciliation** panel.*

> "Finally, the December 2025 view reproduces the original report exactly — the differences are only rounding. The code, data
> pipeline and every monthly refit are in our GitHub repository, so anyone can rebuild this dashboard. Thanks for watching."

---

**Timing tips:** if you run long, shorten the 0:45 section to one sentence. If you run short, press **Play** at the end and let it run
through 2025 for a few seconds while you summarize.
